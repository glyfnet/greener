# Habitat on bushfire-prone land

You can have habitat and still protect your home. On bushfire-prone land, Greener plans follow the NSW Rural Fire Service (RFS) approach: keep fuel low near buildings, break up the vegetation, and place habitat away from the house. Always follow your council's conditions and your own Bush Fire Survival Plan first.

## Layout

- Keep an asset protection zone (APZ) around buildings: an area of low fuel, so fire cannot run along the ground or through the canopy to the house. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Put habitat in a few small "havens" well away from the house, not in one dense block. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Break up the plants with paths, gravel, lawn or low native groundcover. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Put taller plants furthest from the house and step down to low plants near it. Do not let plants touch the walls. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens] [kb:rfs-standards-asset-protection-zones]
- In the APZ, keep tree crowns 2 to 5 metres apart, with no canopy over the house within 2 to 5 metres. Keep native trees and shrubs in clumps covering no more than about a fifth of the area. [kb:rfs-standards-asset-protection-zones]

## Plants

- All plants burn. Choose plants with large, smooth, fleshy leaves near the house, such as Dianella and Lomandra, and keep them watered and free of dead leaves. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Keep oily plants such as eucalypts, melaleucas and tea-trees well away from the house, as single trees with clear ground below. Avoid trees with loose or stringy bark near buildings. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens] [kb:rfs-standards-asset-protection-zones]
- Never plant weeds. Weeds spread after fire and add fuel. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens] [kb:nsw-weedwise-greater-sydney]

## Habitat features that suit fire-prone sites

- Use rocks and stone piles, not deep mulch, as shelter for lizards and insects. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Plant dense or spiky shrubs as refuges for small birds, inside the havens. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- A pond or dam can serve as both habitat and a fire break. [kb:rfs-standards-asset-protection-zones]

## Upkeep (add these hours to the plan)

- Before each fire season: rake and remove dead leaves, prune dead plant material, and clear gutters. [kb:rfs-habitat-in-apz-gardens]
- Remove the lowest tree branches up to 2 metres from the ground, and keep grass short and green. [kb:rfs-standards-asset-protection-zones]
