# How Greener builds and checks a plan

Greener writes a plan in steps. Each step has a tool that does the exact work, so the rules do not depend on how a question is worded.

## Where the species data comes from

Greener asks the Atlas of Living Australia (ALA) which species have been recorded within 10 km of your site. It looks for plants with native records, plants with introduced records, animals, threatened species and species that the state flags as invasive. The numbers are counts of records, not counts of plants or animals. Many records do not say whether a plant is native, so a short list does not mean a species is missing from your area.

## How plants are checked

Before a plant goes into your plan, Greener checks it against the research for your site.

- A plant that ALA records as introduced near your site is not allowed in the plan.
- A plant that the state flags as invasive, or that is on the declared weed list, is not allowed in the plan.
- A plant with native records near your site is allowed. This is the best choice. Greener checks the Atlas for each plant you choose, not just the ones in the report.
- A plant with both native and introduced records nearby is allowed with a warning. Ask your nursery for local stock.
- A plant that is native to Australia but not recorded near your site is allowed with a note. A local native usually does more for local wildlife.
- A plant that is not native to Australia is not allowed. Nor is an Australian native that ALA records as introduced near your site: it is invasive there.

Greener plans never use chemicals: no herbicides, pesticides, poison baits or synthetic fertilisers. Weeds are removed by hand and replaced with local natives.

## How actions are put into phases

Each action has a range of hours, a range of cost, a time of year when it can be done, and the actions it depends on. Greener uses the high end of each range, so the plan still fits if the work takes longer or costs more than expected.

- Phase 0, quick wins: month 1. Small actions of 4 hours or less that cost no more than a tenth of your starting budget.
- Phase 1, prepare and establish: months 1 to 6.
- Phase 2, expand: months 7 to 18.
- Phase 3, mature and monitor: months 19 to 36.

Your free hours each month are your weekly hours, times 4.33 weeks, times 0.8, less the upkeep of actions you have already done. Upkeep keeps going after an action is done, so it takes time away from new work later. An action that does not fit by month 36 goes on the deferred list with the reason: time, money, season or another action it waits for. Greener also shows what 2 more hours a week and half as much budget again would add.

## Costs

All costs are estimates in Australian dollars from a dated price list, and each one shows its date. You can enter your own local prices, and they replace the list for your site. When there is no price for an item, Greener says so and does not make one up.

## Sources and citations

Every action cites at least one source: a guide in the Greener knowledge base, one of your own documents, or the research data for your site. When a guide is a link to another website, Claude reads the live page and cites it. If web access is off, Claude uses Greener's summary and says that it could not read the live page.

## What Greener keeps

The Greener server keeps nothing about you or your site. Your answers, research and plans are saved in your own Claude Project.
