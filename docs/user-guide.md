# User guide

Greener helps you increase biodiversity on your own land in New South Wales. It runs inside Claude, with nothing to install. Your answers, research and plans are saved in your own Claude Project. The Greener server keeps nothing.

Start by making a Claude Project for your site. Then turn on web access, so Claude can read the guides that Greener links to.

## 1. Tell Greener about your site — `/greener-new`

Claude asks about 20 short questions, such as where the site is, what type of site it is, how much time you have and what you can spend. Tap a button, or type the number of your choice. Every question has **Other** for your own answer, and some have **Not sure**.

> **You:** /greener-new
> **Claude:** Question 2 of 20: What type of site is it?
> 1. Apartment or balcony · 2. Townhouse or courtyard · 3. Suburban house · …
> **You:** 3

To change one answer later, just ask: "Change my hours to 2–5 a week."

## 2. Research your area — `/greener-research`

Claude looks up which plants and animals have been recorded within 10 km of your site in the Atlas of Living Australia. It then writes a short report: native plants, wildlife, threatened species, weeds and pests, and any gaps in the data.

## 3. Get a plan — `/greener-plan`

Claude writes a plan with quick wins, actions, a planting list and a way to track progress. The plan fits your real time and money:

- Each action shows its hours, its cost range and when in the year to do it.
- Actions go into phases, from quick wins in month 1 to months 19–36.
- Actions that don't fit are listed with the reason, and with what 2 more hours a week would add.
- Greener is for rewilding: it plants **Australian natives only** (local ones first), never invasive plants, and uses **no chemicals**. Weeds, and plants recorded as introduced near your site, are never allowed. Weeds are removed by hand and the gap is replanted.
- Costs are estimates with a date. Where Greener has no price, it says so.

When you finish an action, tell Claude: "I finished the weeding. It took 6 hours and cost $20." The rest of the plan moves to fit.

## 4. Ask questions — `/greener-ask`

> **You:** /greener-ask Which of my plants will feed small birds?

Answers cite their sources: Greener's guides, your own documents, or your site research. If Greener has no data, it says so.

## 5. Add your own documents — `/greener-add-docs`

Add council lists, nursery catalogues or your own notes to the Project. Claude uses them in plans and answers, and they never leave your Claude account.

## 6. Export — `/greener-export`

Save the plan as Markdown or JSON, with all sources and credits.
